ABOUT RELICS

BUILT BY COLLECTORS, FOR COLLECTORS

Relics is a local die-cast small business born out of a genuine love for miniature automotive history. We specialize in sourcing hard-to-find Hot Wheels mainlines, premium series, pop culture editions, and rare carded die-cast models.

Whether you are a seasoned collector hunting for a Super Treasure Hunt or a beginner starting your first display case, Relics ensures 100% authentic condition and pristine packaging for every die-cast car in our vault.
